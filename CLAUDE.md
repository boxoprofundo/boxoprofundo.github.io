# Working with Mike

## Hand off busywork to Cowork

Mike has a Cowork assistant that can use his real browser and his signed-in accounts. This
session's tools are limited: no logged-in browser, no way to sign in to sites, no access to
his accounts' settings pages.

Whenever a task needs something I cannot do from here (checking a site while logged in,
comparing settings, clicking through an app, gathering information from pages behind a
login, or any research or busywork that is long and manual), do not just tell Mike to go and
do it. Write him a ready-to-paste Cowork prompt in a code block that:

- says he is already signed in and that nothing should be bought, joined or paid for;
- gives the exact URLs, names, dates and times involved;
- lists numbered steps and says exactly what to report back;
- limits any change Cowork may make to the one setting that is needed, and says to report it;
- asks it to say so when a page didn't load or it couldn't tell, instead of guessing.

Then tell him to paste Cowork's answer back, and continue from that.
